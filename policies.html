Two governing policies, in full

Two governing policies (this item, PF8) written for the systems they govern: PF1 Scanner-for-IBMA and PF5 SkyWorks Domain. Those systems belong to external organisations, so internal code and data stay private. The policy texts are my own authorship, published here in full.

Policy for PF1 · Scanner-for-IBMA: data-processing ethics

Governs: autonomous student/contact data scanning for a Registered Training Organisation. v1.0 · 2026-11-08.

Frameworks

Indigenous Data Sovereignty (IDS)

"Ensuring that Indigenous people have a say in the data that is produced about them, how this data is shared and the purpose behind sharing the data" (Kukutai & Taylor, Indigenous Data Sovereignty: Towards An Agenda, ANU Press, 2016). Recognised through UNDRIP (2007), Art. 3 (self-determination) and Art. 31 (cultural heritage, traditional knowledge).

CARE Principles (Global Indigenous Data Alliance, 2019)

Collective benefit · Authority to control · Responsibility · Ethics (Carroll et al., Data Science Journal 13:9, 2020).

AIATSIS Code of Ethics (2020) + Research Ethics Framework

Indigenous self-determination, Indigenous leadership, impact and value, sustainability and accountability.

ACS Code of Professional Ethics (2023)

Honesty, trustworthiness, respect.

Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) + Australian Privacy Principles

APP 2 (pseudonymity), APP 3 (collection), APP 6 (use/disclosure), APP 8 (cross-border), APP 11 (identifiers).

Data inventory & implemented controls

PrincipleControl actually built into the project
Authority to control (CARE) / self-determination (AIATSIS)All data belongs to the institution and its students; output from the tool returns only to the institution's own systems or the operator's machine.
Data minimisation (APP 3)The pipeline extracts only the contact-code column needed to build the ID-keyed workbook.
Pseudonymisation (APP 2, APP 11)Individuals travel as ContactID-* codes; the code→person mapping stays inside the institution's systems and is never duplicated by this tool.
Data residency / no cross-border transfer (APP 8)Processing is local, single-operator. *.txt, *.xlsx and /memory/ are git-ignored (verified); personal data structurally cannot reach any remote repository.
Integrity of student recordsPost-crash behaviour is merge-not-overwrite (merge()); recover.py rebuilds workbooks from exports, verified by the CI smoke test.
Retention & disposalExports are working copies; superseded exports are deleted at term end; backups never leave the operator's machine.
Access controlRuns only under the operator's own authenticated session; no credentials stored in the repository.

Indigenous-specific obligations

No inferred identity data

No Indigenous identity data is inferred or derived by the tool. Institutional records of Aboriginal/Torres Strait Islander status (e.g. support programs) are treated as sensitive: aggregated only, never exported free-text, never used beyond the program that collected them.

Community data ownership

Datasets produced from community partnership programs remain under the partner community's control; publication requires free, prior and informed consent.

Culturally appropriate language

Generated reports use inclusive, non-deficit language; no student is named publicly; community-program terminology follows what the community itself uses.

Nothing published without aggregation

Nothing published: any output requested for public reporting has Indigenous-related data removed or aggregated beyond identification first.

Accountability

Complaints: escalate to the institution, then the OAIC privacy complaint process. Annual review, with mandatory re-review whenever data sources, outputs or hosting change. Suspected disclosure of student data is assessed against the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

Policy for PF5 · SkyWorks Domain: imagery-of-Country & enquiry privacy

Governs: aerial/drone imagery services (Sydney + 300 km) and the static website's enquiry data. v1.0 · 2026-11-08.

Why the ordering

SkyWorks flies and publishes imagery of Country, including land that may hold cultural heritage, ceremonial sites or resting places. The highest ethical duty is to First Nations custodians; the second is visitors' personal information.

Controls actually built into the project

PrincipleControl
Openness & transparency (APP 1)This published policy states what is collected (enquiry emails only) and why (quotes, bookings).
Data minimisation (APP 3)Collection limited to what a quote needs: name, contact, shoot location/scope. No marketing lists, no profiling.
Purpose limitation (APP 6)Enquiry data is used only for the enquiry: never sold, shared or repurposed; retained for the quote-follow-up period, then deleted.
Data residency (APP 8)Static self-hosted site; no third-party data processors, no cross-border transfer of visitor data.
No silent trackingNo analytics/cookie scripts exist in the committed site (verified by repository search). Adding any requires updating this policy first.
Honest representation (ACS 2023)Portfolio imagery represents real work; AI-assisted imagery, if ever used, is labelled as such.

Imagery of Country: Indigenous obligations

Pre-flight cultural screening

Before low-altitude work anywhere, check for registered cultural heritage, sacred sites, ceremonial grounds, sorry-business or access restrictions; in doubt, ground-truth with the Traditional Owner group or land council and obtain permission before capture.

Authority to control publication (CARE / UNDRIP Art. 31)

Cultural heritage, sites and ceremonial features visible in imagery are not published without custodian consent; out of caution, sensitive features are cropped, blurred or withheld by default.

Takedown duty

Any custodian can request removal; imagery is removed on request without requiring proof of ownership, and removals are logged.

No cultural knowledge extraction

No mapping or publishing of knowledge about cultural sites; no site coordinates in metadata or captions; GPS EXIF stripped from published images.

Language & acknowledgement

Site copy about Country uses inclusive language, names Traditional Custodians of worked areas where known, and carries an Acknowledgement of Country.

Deceased persons & gender-restricted business

Not published without clearance.

Accountability

Takedown and privacy requests via the published enquiry address; unresolved privacy complaints to the OAIC. Annual review, and mandatory re-review before any new flight region, hosting/CRM service, or imagery class.

Versioning

Both documents are v1.0, authored 8 November 2026. Each carries an annual review cycle and mandatory re-review whenever data sources, outputs, hosting or scope change.
